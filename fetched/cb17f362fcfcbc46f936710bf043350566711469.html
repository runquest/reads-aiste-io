    MARK CUBAN REVEALED ONE OF HIS COMPANIES IS SAVING $50,000 A MONTH USING AN AI AGENT THAT JUST TAKES PICTURES. “ONE OF MY SHARK TANK COMPANIES IS CALLED REBELCHEESE.COM. THEY SELL VEGAN CHEESE AND SHIP IT ALL OVER THE WORLD, AND THEY'RE LIKE A LOT OF COMPANIES THAT SHIP PRODUCTS IN BOXES.” “YOU KNOW THERE'S DIFFERENT SIZE BOXES FOR UPS OR DHL, AND THERE'S A DIFFERENT PRICE TO SHIP TO A DIFFERENT ZONE. THEY DON'T HAVE TO TOUCH IT.”
