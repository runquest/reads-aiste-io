## For anyone who's not Canadian, something you should know is that we're weirdly proud of a few things: [_Heated Rivalry_](https://www.buzzfeed.com/jennifer_mcphee/heated-rivalry-sexiest-show-to-watch-reactions) (duh), [Ryan Gosling](https://www.buzzfeed.com/noradominick/spring-movie-preview-2026), [Joni Mitchell](https://www.buzzfeed.com/jennifer_mcphee/joni-mitchell-wins-juno-lifetime-achievement-award), Purdys chocolate, and…[Canadian Tire](https://www.canadiantire.ca/en/pdp/canadian-tire-branded-plaid-reusable-bag-11x14-5x10-4-in-2425514p.html).

## If you've never heard of it, Canadian Tire is our ultimate hardware store...that also fixes cars. You can buy patio furniture, pick up lumber for your next DIY Pinterest inspiration, get windshield-wiping fluid...all while a mechanic changes your tires. It's basically Home Depot with a garage and Canadian flags.

  ![Canadian Tire store exterior with large signage visible](https://img.buzzfeed.com/buzzfeed-static/static/2026-08/05/14/subbuzz/tTC9FEehY.jpg?downsize=700%3A%2A&output-quality=auto&output-format=auto)

NicolasMcComber / Getty Images

## And Canadian [_Heated_](https://www.buzzfeed.com/jennifer_mcphee/heated-rivalry-ilya-or-shane-personality-quiz) [_Rivalry_](https://www.buzzfeed.com/jennifer_mcphee/heated-rivalry-ilya-or-shane-personality-quiz) star [Hudson William](https://www.buzzfeed.com/jennifer_mcphee/heated-rivalry-lead-actors-connor-storrie-hudson-williams) only had to walk around with an item from Canadian Tire for one afternoon with his costar, [Connor Storrie](https://www.buzzfeed.com/noradominick/heated-rivalry-character-quiz), to make it HOT. And the item will COMPLETELY shock you. It wasn't a hockey stick, a power drill, or a pair of hiking boots — it was a reusable shopping bag. More specifically, a red plaid Canadian Tire tote bag.

  ![Reusable shopping bag with a plaid pattern and a &quot;Canadian Tire&quot; logo on the side](https://img.buzzfeed.com/buzzfeed-static/static/2026-08/05/13/subbuzz/96mk5mC_R.png?downsize=700%3A%2A&output-quality=auto&output-format=auto)

Canadian Tire

## [According to CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/entertainment/hudson-williams-canadian-tire-tote-9.7295988), Canadian Tire employees have already started calling it the "[Hudson Williams bag](https://x.com/deuxmoiworld/status/2083632947647631653/photo/1)." And if you're thinking you can just pop into a store and grab one for yourself, think again. Because according to CBC, _every_ store across the Greater Toronto Area has completely sold out.

## Turns out, it wasn't even _remotely_ popular before the paparazzi photos of Connor and Hudson. A Canadian Tire employee told CBC, "I was very shocked when I heard, 'Oh, that one got trendy.'" The employee went on to say that the bag had been a "slow mover" in sales, and the store had been trying to clear its inventory because hardly anyone was buying it. Enter...Hudson Williams.

![Three people standing in an aisle of a store, two wearing aprons and one holding a microphone, appearing to conduct an interview](https://img.buzzfeed.com/buzzfeed-static/static/2026-08/05/14/subbuzz/GvOrBv1kE.png)

## And once the photo started making the rounds online, some Canadians were quick to take advantage of the opportunity. One Canadian Tire employee told CBC, "There was a guy actually who first came in, and he said some famous guy had a bag or something, and how it's going to sell out quick. So he came and bought five bags." Honestly, that was a pretty smart move. As a Toronto girl, I kind of wish I could get my hands on one of those totes now.
