- Cursor, the AI coding IDE built by four MIT grads into $4B annual revenue and 50,000 enterprise customers, is being acquired for $60 billion
- The acquisition will result in Cursor's name disappearing, with its unreleased agent shipped under a Grok label instead
- Cursor's model-agnostic approach attracted users away from Copilot, but the acquisition demonstrates that app-layer companies without their own AI models cannot remain independent
