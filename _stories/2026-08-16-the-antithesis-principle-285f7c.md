(1) If you want to help people learn something, look into making it engaging / entertaining. This first one is the easier, more obvious conclusion. (2) It is actually not in your best interest to be the kind of person who can only learn from entertaining content. Nor to be the kind of person that needs any sort of entertainment as a prerequisite for learning. The vast majority of smart people will never consider this second conclusion.
