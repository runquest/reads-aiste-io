Prioritization is a trap. It wastes time, wastes effort, and delivers worse results than just executing faster. Instead of spending your time prioritizing: (1) Spend more time focusing on building faster. (2) Shift everyone into durable teams that don't have to do cross-business prioritization at all, and manage your "prioritization" by the resourcing decisions of growing, downsizing, or splitting teams.
