Microsoft and LinkedIn data show 52% of workers hesitate to admit using AI for important tasks, while 78% bring their own AI tools to work. Inc. argues mixed signals from leaders are creating "shadow AI," making mistakes harder to catch. The proposed fix is clearer policies, transparent leadership, rewards for judgment, and no-blame reporting when AI fails.
