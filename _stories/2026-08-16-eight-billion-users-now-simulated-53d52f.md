- Harvard and MIT team released MatrAIx, a system that creates 8.3 billion synthetic 'users' stitched from real-world data for product testing
- AI models can now simulate human behavior in surveys, app usage, and browsing, collapsing months of user research into minutes
- Same persona yields dramatically different results across models (23% to 94% purchase intent variance), raising concerns about whose voice is actually represented
