Prioritization frameworks waste time and rarely prove their worth. Instead, build faster: speed beats clever decision-making every time, and shipping more teaches you what works. Keep teams focused on one area permanently so decisions stay simple and comparable. Treat resourcing as your main lever, not constant replanning.
