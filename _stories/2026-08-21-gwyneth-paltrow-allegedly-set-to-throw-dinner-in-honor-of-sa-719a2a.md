# Gwyneth Paltrow allegedly set to throw dinner in honor of Sam Altman

Sam Altman is headed to the Hamptons, [according to Puck](https://puck.news/newsletter_content/what-im-hearing-netflixs-dc-muscle-igers-lakers-angst-gwyneths-ai-party/). Gwyneth Paltrow is reportedly sending invites for a “private, off-the-record” dinner honoring the tech mogul on August 29 at her home out East. 

Paltrow is [an actress and founder of the wellness brand Goop.](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/20/gwyneth-paltrow-says-goop-is-growing-just-fine/) But she’s also a noted investor through her Kinship Ventures, which she launched in 2021 alongside Moj Mahdara. In 2023, reports said [the firm was raising a $75 million fund](https://www.axios.com/2023/03/02/scoop-gwyneth-paltrow-is-raising-a-75-million-venture-capital-fund), though it remains unclear whether it has closed. Kinship is also an [investor in OpenAI](https://www.kinshipventures.co/) (per SEC filings, her investment in OpenAI [dates back to 2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1974366/000189025723000049/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml)) and other AI companies, including the vibe-coding tool Lovable and the AI-powered [marketing platform Nectar Social](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/marketing-operating-system-nectar-social-raises-30m-series-a-in-round-led-by-menlo/). 

We’ve reached out to Goop and OpenAI for comment. Over the past few years, Paltrow has become more outspoken about AI — she’s spoken [about](https://www.businessinsider.com/gwyneth-paltrow-ai-era-tech-goop-new-obsession-2026-8) how she uses the tools to run her company, and her Goop Kitchen [has signed a lease for the ground floor](https://www.costar.com/article/368165189/gwyneth-paltrows-goop-kitchen-is-getting-in-on-the-us-tech-boom) of Anthropic’s San Francisco HQ. Earlier this year, OpenAI hired Charles Porch, whom Vanity Fair called [the “celebrity whisperer](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/influencers-draw-backlash-for-attending-openais-first-luxury-trip/).”
