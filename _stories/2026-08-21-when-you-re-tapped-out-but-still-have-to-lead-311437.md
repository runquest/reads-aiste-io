Tiredness is normal in leadership, but there is a deeper level where pushing through causes real damage instead of growth. When you hit that point, cut your workload, respect your natural pace, and rest before a small problem becomes a long burnout. Your team watches how you handle your own limits, so modeling sustainable habits matters as much as any decision you make.
