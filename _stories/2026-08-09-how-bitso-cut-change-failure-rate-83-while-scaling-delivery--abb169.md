Bitso's 250+ engineers and their coding agents push changes across 200+ microservices, and shared staging was always broken. They replaced full-stack preview environments with Signadot sandboxes that fork only the changed services per PR. The result: 83% lower change failure rate, 2.1x deploy frequency, and open-to-deploy down from 12.1 days to 4.6 days.
