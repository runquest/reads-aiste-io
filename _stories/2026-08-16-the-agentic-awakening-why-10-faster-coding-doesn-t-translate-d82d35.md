Even companies with 85-90% AI-generated code and 10x local acceleration never crossed 50% end-to-end productivity gain, because the organization around the engineers, PM handoffs, planning cadence, and review queues still runs at pre-AI speed. Individual velocity is the easy win; redesigning the org so faster engineers actually ship faster is the part almost everyone skips.
